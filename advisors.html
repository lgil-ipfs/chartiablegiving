How It Works

Four steps to a better client conversation.

1

Identify the opportunity

During your next annual review, ask your client: "Have you thought about incorporating charitable giving into your financial plan?" Many clients have charitable intentions but have never been asked directly. This simple question opens the door.

2

Share the right resource

Point clients to charitablegiving.ca — whether that's the giving guide, the charity finder, or a specific article on securities donations or legacy giving that matches their situation. Our content is designed to educate, not to sell.

3

Build the strategy together

Work with your client to identify the right giving vehicles for their situation — which accounts hold appreciated securities, whether a DAF or direct giving is more efficient, how their estate plan can be updated to reflect charitable intentions.

4

Execute and review annually

Build charitable giving into your annual review process. Review year-end giving, tax receipts, any needed updates to estate documents, and whether the client's charitable priorities have evolved. This is a lifelong relationship, not a one-time transaction.
